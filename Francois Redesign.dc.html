TURN 4 · MULTI-ACCOUNT

One account per session

Built on 3a. Each account gets a fixed identity — a tinted initial tile plus its own quota meter — and every session carries the tile of the account it burns. The titlebar shows only the active session's quota so the number in front of you always belongs to the work in front of you; the rail holds the whole roster. When an account hits its limit the sessions on it pause and the pane offers to reroute rather than silently stalling.

TURN 3 · AGENT TABS

Agents as tabs, not a panel

Built on 2a. The tab strip splits in two: the four fixed views stay in a segmented group, then a divider, then one tab per live agent — spawned in, closable, each carrying its own status dot. Opening an agent tab replaces the transcript with that agent's own trace, so the right rail stops being a viewer and becomes a launcher: a roster with counts, not a second reading surface. Overflow collapses to a +N chip rather than shrinking the fixed tabs — here the third agent sits behind +1.
